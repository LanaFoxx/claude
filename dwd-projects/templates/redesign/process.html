Step by step

How your website gets built.

Here is what to expect, from our first conversation through launch day and beyond. Six steps, one person handling all of them.

The process

From first call to launch.

Nothing goes live until you approve it. You see every page before it’s published.

  1. 01
    Discovery call

    We begin with a Zoom call where you tell me about your business, your customers and your goals for the website, and I ask the questions that shape the project’s direction.

    What you receive: a clear quote and timeline, explained without pressure or technical jargon.

  2. 02
    One onboarding form

    Rather than a long email thread, you complete one concise onboarding form. It covers your business, goals, branding, content, domain and timeline, and lets you upload your logo, photos and files in one place.

    Still gathering materials? That’s completely normal. Let me know what’s missing, and we’ll work through it together.

  3. 03
    Home page first

    I design your home page first because it establishes the look, feel and messaging for the entire site. We refine it together, with unlimited revisions, until it’s exactly right.

    Why it works: once the home page is approved, every other page follows the same direction, which keeps the project consistent and free of surprises.

  4. 04
    Page by page

    With the home page approved, I build the remaining pages one at a time, and you review and approve each one before I proceed.

    You stay in control: nothing goes live until you’ve reviewed and approved it.

  5. 05
    Launch together

    Before launch, we test every page, form and link on desktop, tablet and mobile. For redesigns, I preserve your existing URLs and SEO settings so your search rankings carry over.

    Then we launch: your site goes live, and I lead a one-on-one training session so you can manage and update it with confidence.

  6. 06
    Ongoing support

    Launch is only the beginning. Every site includes two months of complimentary support and a lifetime workmanship warranty, and hosting clients receive ongoing updates, backups, security and monitoring.

    Need something later? Call or text me directly, and you’ll reach the person who built your site.

Before we start

What to have ready.

You don’t need everything finalized before we begin. The items below are helpful, and the onboarding form guides you through each one.

Your logo and brand

Your logo files and any brand colors or fonts. If you don’t have a logo yet, we can discuss your options.

Your content

The text for each page, or rough notes I can refine if you’d like help with the writing.

Photos and videos

Photos of your business, team, products or work. If you need help sourcing images, I can assist.

Websites you like

Two or three websites you admire and what appeals to you about each. This is one of the most valuable references you can share.

Domain and hosting details

The provider for your domain and email. If you’re unsure, I’ll help you find out.
