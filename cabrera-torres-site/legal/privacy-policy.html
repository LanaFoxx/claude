Cabrera Torres Law Firm (“Cabrera Torres Law Firm,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through cabreratorres.com and our client communications, including text messages, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the date of your accident or injury, and any details you share about your situation when you fill out our contact form, call, email, or text us.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as your browser type, device type, pages visited, and IP address, collected through cookies and similar tools that help our website work and help us understand how it is used.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and evaluate your potential case.
  • To communicate with you about your case, including case updates, appointment reminders, and document requests.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website.
  • To comply with our legal and professional obligations.

Submitting a contact form or sending us information does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship. We treat the information you share with care and confidentiality consistent with our professional obligations.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your consent, we may send you text messages about your case, such as case updates, appointment reminders, and document requests. We do not use text messaging for marketing.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of hiring us or of receiving any service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates. The information-sharing categories described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

See our SMS Terms for full details of our text messaging program.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us run our website, phone, and case-management systems, and who are required to keep it confidential and use it only to provide services to us. This does not include text messaging opt-in data and consent, which is never shared.
  • When required by law, court order, or professional rules, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • With your permission, or as needed to pursue your case (for example, with courts, insurers, or experts on your behalf).

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to function properly and to understand how visitors use it. You can set your browser to refuse or delete cookies; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may ask us to update or correct your contact information, or ask questions about the information we hold, by contacting us below.

Children’s privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
