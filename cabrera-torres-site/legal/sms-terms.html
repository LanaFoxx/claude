Program description

Cabrera Torres Law Firm sends text messages to clients and prospective clients who have agreed to receive them. Messages relate to your case only: case updates, appointment reminders, and document requests. We do not send marketing text messages.

How you opt in

You can opt in by checking the optional text message consent box on our website contact form, or by giving us your consent directly (for example, by phone or in writing). Consent is not a condition of hiring us or of receiving any service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your case. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts unless you opt in again.
  • Reply HELP for help, call 770-777-HURT, or email info@cabreratorres.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates. For more, see our Privacy Policy.
