Snow Bentley

Global Reach, Local Expertise

Specializing in Commercial Sales & Leasing
Luxury Residential Real Estate

Snow Bentley believes the best deals aren't won - they're made. As founder of Snow Bentley Realty, Inc., brokered by eXp Commercial, Snow works across two worlds: commercial leasing for restaurant operators, retail brands, business owners, and investors, and luxury residential for buyers and sellers throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. Through her brokerage network, she also partners with agents worldwide.

Snow approaches real estate the way a business owner does, because she has been one. Before she earned her real estate license more than a decade ago, she ran an import-export company, invested in startups, and owned and operated a restaurant in Santa Barbara. She knows what it takes to sign a lease, build out a space, and keep the doors open. That is why food and beverage and retail clients trust her to find locations that work for their business, not just ones that look good on paper.
