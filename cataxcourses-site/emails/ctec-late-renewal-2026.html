Good to know: what happens after January 15

If your renewal isn’t completed by January 15, 2027, your CTEC registration lapses. To prepare taxes in California again you would need to register as a new preparer, which means taking the 60 Hour Qualifying Education course again. Renewing now keeps your registration active and saves you that extra time.
