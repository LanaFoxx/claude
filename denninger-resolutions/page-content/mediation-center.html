By Zoom, with the same privacy as the suite.

For parties across the state, insurance representatives joining from out of town, or anyone for whom travel does not make sense.

  • Private breakout rooms for caucusEach side has its own room; I move between them. Nobody hears the other side's caucus.
  • Waiting room on, recording offI admit participants by name. Sessions are never recorded, consistent with DRC Advisory Opinion 43.
  • Optional tech checkA ten-minute call the week before for any participant who wants one, especially parties who have not used Zoom.
  • Signing at a distanceSettlement terms are circulated for electronic signature before the session ends.

Accommodations

Tell me what a party needs, and I will plan the day around it.

Interpreters, mobility or hearing needs, staggered arrivals so parties do not cross paths, extra breaks, a support person in the room, or a session paced for someone who finds the subject hard to talk about. Note it in the booking form, or email David@DenningerResolutions.com and I will confirm the arrangements with you before the date. Requests stay confidential.
