Why counsel select me

A trial lawyer's read on risk. A steady hand in the room.

Lawyers select a mediator both sides will trust, who knows what a case is worth at trial, and who can keep hard conversations at the table. Here is what I bring to each.

A trial lawyer's read

Years in Superior Court give me a realistic view of evidence, risk and juries. I share that read candidly, and I never predict outcomes.

Steady with hard cases

Much of my career has been spent with victims and people in crisis. Injured or grieving parties get time, plain explanations and a quiet room.

Credible to both sides

I have prepared and tried cases as a prosecutor and as defense counsel. Neither side gets a lean, and both get the same candor and the same time.

About David

A trial lawyer, now a neutral.

I grew up in Western North Carolina and served in the United States Army from 2004 to 2010 before law school at Wake Forest. Admitted to the North Carolina bar in 2014, I spent about ten years (2014 to 2024) as an Assistant District Attorney in Buncombe and McDowell Counties, trying cases in District and Superior Court.

Since 2024 I have been in private practice in Asheville, and in 2025 the NC Dispute Resolution Commission certified me to conduct Superior Court mediated settlement conferences. I live in Asheville with my family.
