Fees and policies

Plain terms, stated in advance.

Fees are paid by the parties at the conclusion of the conference unless the court orders otherwise. Ability to pay is never discussed before a conference is scheduled or held.

Printable fees and policies (PDF)
Party-selected mediations$350 per hour, plus a $150 administrative fee per case. Preparation time is included. Travel outside Buncombe County is billed at the hourly rate.
Court-appointed mediations$150 per hour, plus a one-time $150 administrative fee, as set by Rule 7 of the Superior Court MSC rules.
How fees are splitEqual shares among the parties unless otherwise agreed or ordered. Each party pays its own share and receives its own receipt.
When fees are dueAt the conclusion of the conference unless otherwise agreed or ordered. Nothing is due to book a date.
Postponement and cancellationPostponements or cancellations with fewer than five business days’ notice are billed at two hours of the hourly rate, divided among the parties responsible, unless the case has settled.
Paying electronicallyA party who chooses to pay by card covers the card processing fee, shown before payment, as DRC Advisory Opinion 44 permits. Bank transfer (ACH) carries no fee. Checks are also accepted.
